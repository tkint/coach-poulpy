# Screens

Every screen mockup is a story in the [component workshop](?path=/docs/design-system-introduction--docs) ([ADR 0029](https://tkint.github.io/coach-poulpy/docs/adr/0029-mockups-are-stories-in-the-component-workshop)): the web screens under Web > Screens, composed from the `Cp*` components, and the phone screens under Mobile > Screens, built from the `Mobile*` components and patterns.

- Build from an agreed mockup. If a screen has none, agree one through a PR before building it.
- Each state of a screen is a story, in light and dark from the workshop's theme toolbar. A new screen or redesign is tagged `draft`; a PR that changes the tag to `agreed` is its approval, and nothing is built from a draft.
- CI runs every story as a test, with the accessibility check.

The mockup is the design reference; this page defines the shared rules and components used to build it.

## Navigation

The bottom bar has three destinations: **Home**, **Library** and **You**. Live is the **Record** button in Home's top bar, which opens a bottom sheet; History is the past feed on Home; Statistics sit on You under the profile card, and Settings stays **Settings**, behind the cog in You's top bar. See Mobile > Screens > Home, Library and You.

- Home is the diary: the motivation row, **Up next** (what is planned, or a suggestion when nothing is), then the past with a recap or a record between sessions.
- The profile card on You carries one badge: **Guest**, **Confirm your email** or **Signed in**. Sign up and sign in open from the one Account row at the top of the profile (it opens the account screens), never from a banner or a pop-up.
- Statistics have a period switch (7 days, 30 days, Year, All time) and an Edit button that chooses and orders the blocks.
