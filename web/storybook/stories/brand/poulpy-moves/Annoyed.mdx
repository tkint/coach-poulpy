# Poulpy annoyed

The ember side, not yet angry. A draft for Home to validate: how he answers a tap while he is annoyed, with no transition. Until now a tap does nothing at this stage, so these are proposals. He is annoyed when the page opens; tap him in the picture, or pick a variant.

<Story of={Live.Annoyed} />

- **Shake**: a quick shake of the head and body, side to side, without leaving the floor.
- **Stomp**: a small stamp, he drops a little and lands with a bounce.
- **Huff**: he puffs up, then lets it out with a small lean.

None of them is a turn: the turn is kept for changing mood. They are the same little move as the fuming tucks, much smaller, and he has no fumes yet. The spins in and out of annoyed are on [Getting angry](?path=/docs/brand-poulpy-moves-getting-angry--docs). Pain and rehab never show the ember side, so they never come in here.
