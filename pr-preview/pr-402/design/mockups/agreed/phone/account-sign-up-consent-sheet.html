What is stored

Once your email is confirmed, your phone sends this to Coach Poulpy’s server so you can find it on other phones:

  • Workouts, plans and sessions
  • Your profile and body measurements, including pain or rehab
  • Units and language
Theme, app icon and paired devices stay on each phone. Why: to back up and sync. Nothing is shared with anyone, and you can delete the account and everything in it whenever you like. Until you confirm your email, nothing leaves this phone.
