`CpDialog`: a centred dialog for a review the person confirms, with a title, a line of help, a close button, the content and a row of actions. `wide` gives a long table room; `rising` makes it rise from the bottom on a phone-width page.

| Vue | Classes |
| --- | --- |
| `<CpDialog>` | `dialog.cp-modal` (`.cp-modal-bottom.sm:cp-modal-middle` when `rising`) and `.cp-modal-box`, `.cp-modal-backdrop` |
