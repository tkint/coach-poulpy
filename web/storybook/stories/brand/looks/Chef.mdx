# Poulpy the cook

**Draft, to choose from.** Poulpy dressed up as a cook who is sure of everything: a tall white toque worn a little askew and a curled French moustache. Two faces, side by side, so the owner can pick one.

<Story of={Live.Chef} />

- **A · Cocky** is his cocky face (the proud mood's): one lid down and one brow up, with its smirk moved under the moustache and his eyes a little higher to make room.
- **B · Big grin** is a wide, clenched, toothy grin from cheek to cheek, eyes wide open and both brows up: the laugh of a cook who has never burnt a sauce. It is our own drawing, made for Poulpy.
- **The toque is solid.** A plain band, then a puff with soft folds and a light crease in each. It sits on his head, leans 9° to his left and 5° back, and goes wherever his head goes.
- **The moustache is part of his face.** A handlebar in his ink, thick under his middle and curling up at both tips, pressed onto his head like his eyes and brows, so it stays on his skin when he turns. On the grin it lies over the top teeth.
- **His idle is small.** He stands still, then puffs himself up: his chin goes up 6° and his head grows a little taller, on a spring, and while he holds it his moustache twitches twice, tips up. Then he settles back. `CHEF` in `motion.mjs` holds the timing.
- **Still Poulpy.** Proud, on the blue side, eight tentacles on the floor and no arms (brand decision 8). Turn him with the slider: the toque and the moustache stay where they belong.

The look is in [`design/poulpy-3d/looks/chef.mjs`](https://github.com/tkint/coach-poulpy/blob/main/design/poulpy-3d/looks/chef.mjs): `TOQUE`, `MOUSTACHE` and `CHEF_FACES`. It is a preview only: not in the glTF files, the pictures or the apps.
