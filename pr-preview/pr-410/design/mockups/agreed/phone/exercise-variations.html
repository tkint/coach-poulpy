Diamond push-up

MusclesTriceps, chest, shoulders
EquipmentNone

How to do it

  1. 1Place your hands under your chest, thumbs and index fingers touching in a diamond.
  2. 2Body straight from head to heels, lower your chest to your hands, elbows close to your sides.
  3. 3Push back up.
