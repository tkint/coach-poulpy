# Coach Poulpy design system

The app is calm and rounded, like the plush: one blue accent for actions, white or near-white surfaces, and only Poulpy and the Home header tint with his mood. Values live in [`design/tokens.json`](https://github.com/tkint/coach-poulpy/blob/main/design/tokens.json) and are generated into each platform (see [`design/README.md`](https://github.com/tkint/coach-poulpy/blob/main/design/README.md)); these rules say how to use them. The live swatches are on the [Tokens](?path=/docs/design-system-tokens--docs) page of the component workshop.

The landing site, the web app and these pages draw with one package, [`web/ui`](https://github.com/tkint/coach-poulpy/tree/main/web/ui): DaisyUI with the Poulpy theme and a few Vue components on top ([ADR 0023](https://tkint.github.io/coach-poulpy/docs/adr/0023-the-websites-share-one-vue-and-daisyui-design-system)). This workshop is its home ([ADR 0028](https://tkint.github.io/coach-poulpy/docs/adr/0028-storybook-is-the-component-workshop-of-the-design-system), [ADR 0029](https://tkint.github.io/coach-poulpy/docs/adr/0029-mockups-are-stories-in-the-component-workshop)).

- **Rules**: how to use [colour](?path=/docs/design-system-rules-colour--docs), [type](?path=/docs/design-system-rules-type--docs), [space, radius and motion](?path=/docs/design-system-rules-space-radius-and-motion--docs), [icons](?path=/docs/design-system-rules-icons--docs), [accessibility](?path=/docs/design-system-rules-accessibility--docs), the [components](?path=/docs/design-system-rules-components--docs), the [screens](?path=/docs/design-system-rules-screens--docs) and the [websites](?path=/docs/design-system-rules-websites--docs).
- [Tokens](?path=/docs/design-system-tokens--docs): every value, read from `design/tokens.json`.
- **Core**: what is the same on every platform: Poulpy and the body map. They are drawn here with the web components.
- **Web > Components**: each Vue component in every state, with its classes. Each state is a story and runs as a test in CI, with an accessibility check. Use the toolbar to switch between light and dark.
- **Mobile > Components**: the raw pieces of the Android and iOS screens (buttons, chips, fields, rows), one page each, with a story per variation and controls to try them.
- **Mobile > Patterns**: pieces composed from those, such as the nav bar, sheets and charts.
- **Mobile > Screens**: every agreed phone screen, built from the components and patterns, and the drafts ([ADR 0029](https://tkint.github.io/coach-poulpy/docs/adr/0029-mockups-are-stories-in-the-component-workshop)).

## Classes

Every DaisyUI class carries the `cp-` prefix (`cp-btn`, not `btn`); the few classes of our own (`cp-chip`, `cp-stepper`, `cp-type-*`...) never reuse a DaisyUI name. Tailwind's own utilities (`w-full`, `bg-secondary`) have no prefix. A component's docs page lists the classes it renders.

Type has plain classes and no component: `cp-type-display`, `cp-type-title`, `cp-type-headline`, `cp-type-label`.

# Colour

## Colour roles

Use roles, never raw palette steps, in screens. Each role has a light and a dark value.

| Role | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| `primary` / `onPrimary` | The main action on a screen (Start session, Save), selected chips, links, progress |
| `primaryContainer` / `onPrimaryContainer` | Tonal buttons, selected rows, Poulpy's speech on the blue side |
| `grumpy` / `onGrumpy` | The one action offered while Poulpy is on the ember side (Catch up, Flip him back) |
| `grumpyContainer` / `onGrumpyContainer` | The Home header and cards while Poulpy is on the ember side |
| `background`, `surface`, `surfaceVariant` | Screen, cards and sheets, inset fields and steppers |
| `onSurfaceVariant`, `outline` | Secondary text and captions; card borders and dividers |
| `success` | A confirmed save or a finished phase, with a check icon |
| `error` / `onError` | Errors only: crimson, always with an icon and words |
| `heat1`, `heat2`, `heat3` | How much a workout works each muscle on the body map, from a little to most. Orange, on the body map only: never for Poulpy's moods |
| `pain` | A worked area that hurts, outlined on the body map. Purple, so it reads apart from the heat and is never mistaken for an error |

Rules:

- `poulpy.blue`, `poulpy.ember` and `poulpy.ink` are Poulpy's fabric and never change between light and dark.
- The plush blue and ember are too light for white text: buttons use `primary` and `grumpy`.
- Ember means "a session slipped", never "something broke". An error is crimson, so a cross Poulpy is never read as a failure.
- Colours that must be told apart also differ in lightness, not hue alone.


## Mood shades

Six shades, from `mood.*`, only on Poulpy himself, the Home header tint and mood-coloured charts:

| Side | Shades, calmest to strongest |
| --- | --- |
| Blue (on track) | `recovering`, `happy`, `proud` |
| Ember (a session slipped) | `annoyed`, `grumpy`, `fuming` |

Which shade shows when is in [Brand](?path=/docs/brand-moods--docs).

# Type

Baloo 2 for display and titles, Nunito for everything else. Platforms map each role to a system text style so Dynamic Type and font scaling keep working (`.font(.poulpy(.headline))` on iOS, `MaterialTheme.typography` on Android).

| Role | Font | Size / line | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `display` | Baloo 2 800 | 40 / 44 | Poulpy's line on Home, end-of-session headline |
| `title` | Baloo 2 700 | 28 / 32 | Screen titles, workout name on its page |
| `headline` | Baloo 2 700 | 21 / 25 | Section heads, the current exercise in the player |
| `body` | Nunito 500 | 16 / 24 | Everything you read |
| `label` | Nunito 800, tracked 0.08 em, upper case | 12.5 / 16 | Eyebrows, tab labels, "Next up" |
| `timer` | Nunito 800, tabular figures | 64 / 64 | Timers and rep counts, which must not jitter |

**Words never break.** Translations run longer than English (« Bibliothèque », « Échauffement »), so text is laid out for any length:

- Text in a space of fixed width (tab labels, chips, stat tiles, stepper values) stays on one line and shrinks to fit, never below 9 sp / pt; past that it ends in an ellipsis. `FitText` on Android, `.lineLimit(1).minimumScaleFactor(…)` on iOS, whose tab bar already does it.
- Buttons wrap their label between words rather than shrink it.
- Every other text wraps between words only, never inside one, and never hyphenates.

# Space, radius and motion

## Space, radius, motion

- Spacing steps: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48 (`xs` to `xxxl`). Pick a step; never a value between them.
- Radius: 8 for chips and small fields, 10 for cards and rows, 14 for sheets, pill for buttons. Small cards stay close to square so their text reads first.
- Controls stay compact: a standard button is 44 high, a large one 52, an icon button 40 (its touch area stays 44), a small button 34. The side gutter of a screen is 16, and rows are 56 high with a 32 to 40 avatar. In the apps these come from one place, `PoulpySize` (generated from `design/tokens.json`, next to `PoulpySpace` and `PoulpyRadius`); a screen never writes a literal height, gutter or corner radius.
- Motion: the flip (700 ms with overshoot) is the one signature move, used only when Poulpy changes side. Mood shades fade in 300 ms. Everything else is 150 to 250 ms and plain. With reduced motion on, the loader slows to one swing in 12 s.

# Icons

Every icon is from [Lucide](https://lucide.dev), on every platform ([ADR 0024](https://tkint.github.io/coach-poulpy/docs/adr/0024-lucide-is-the-icon-set)). Use its name for the action, never a home-made drawing of one Lucide already has. Icons take their colour from the roles above, and an error keeps its icon. Lucide's drawing stays as it is: a 24 box and a 2 wide round stroke, never filled; only the size changes (24 by default, 20 in chips and small buttons, 16 to 18 beside a line of text).

| Where | How |
| --- | --- |
| Vue (web app, `web/ui`, docs site) | `@lucide/vue`: `<ChevronLeft class="size-5" aria-hidden="true" />` |

| Close, clear | `x` |
| More actions | `ellipsis-vertical` |

# Accessibility

- Touch targets at least 44 by 44; a visually smaller control, such as the 40 icon button, extends its touch area to 44.
- Text contrast at least 4.5:1, including small grey labels; 3:1 only for text 24 px and up.
- Every icon-only button has a label; decorative drawings of Poulpy are hidden from screen readers, and a pose that carries meaning has a description.
- Nothing depends on colour alone: mood changes also change Poulpy's face and words, errors carry an icon.

# Websites

The landing site, the web app, the web screens of the workshop and the docs site share one package, `web/ui`: DaisyUI with the Poulpy theme and a few Vue components ([ADR 0023](https://tkint.github.io/coach-poulpy/docs/adr/0023-the-websites-share-one-vue-and-daisyui-design-system)). - Use the shared components (`CpButton`, `CpChip`...) in Vue, and never restyle a DaisyUI class in an app. A missing shape goes in `web/ui`, with its stories and the classes it renders in its docs, in the same PR.
- Every DaisyUI class carries the `cp-` prefix (`cp-btn cp-btn-primary`). Our own classes (`cp-chip`, `cp-stepper`, `cp-type-label`...) never reuse a DaisyUI name, so the two cannot clash.
- The roles above keep their meaning: `cp-btn-primary` is `primary`, `cp-btn-secondary` is the tonal `primaryContainer`, `cp-btn-accent` is `grumpy` and only for the catch-up action. Never use DaisyUI's `warning` classes: ember is not a warning, and a problem is an `error`.
- Phone and tablet mockups keep their own stylesheet (`web/storybook/stories/mobile/screens/screens.css`): they draw the native screens, which DaisyUI's look does not match.
- Icons on the websites and in every screen come from Lucide, as in the [Icons](?path=/docs/design-system-rules-icons--docs) section ([ADR 0024](https://tkint.github.io/coach-poulpy/docs/adr/0024-lucide-is-the-icon-set)).


## Web sizes

Buttons, fields and icons on the websites share one size scale, set with the `size` prop of the `Cp*` components: `xs`, `sm`, `md`, `lg`, `xl`, `xxl`. A component without a `size` is `md`. The numbers come from `webSize` in `design/tokens.json` (`--cp-control-*` and `--cp-icon-*` in the generated `tokens.css`); never write a literal height or icon size.

| Size | Button and field height | Icon (`CpIcon`) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `xs` | 24 | 12 |
| `sm` | 32 | 16 |
| `md` | 40 (the default) | 20 |
| `lg` | 48 | 24 |
| `xl` | 56 | 32 |
| `xxl` | 64 | 40 |

- Which components take `size`: `CpButton`, `CpTextField`, `CpPasswordField`, `CpSearchField`, `CpSelectField`, `CpMultiSelectField`, `CpChipSelectField`, `CpPagination`, `CpSegmentedControl`, and `CpIcon` for every Lucide icon. Each has a Sizes story in the workshop with a size control.
- A screen's main action is `xl` (56), a card's actions and form fields are `md`, a list's own search is `lg`, and the controls of a filter bar or a table's pagination are `sm`. A control's own icons follow its size.
- Draw an icon with `<CpIcon :icon="Search" size="sm" />`, never a Lucide component with a `size-*` class.
- `CpChip`, `CpAvatar`, `CpBadge`, `CpTextarea` and `CpStepper` keep their own sizes for now.

# Character

## Character rules

- **Grumpy is comic, never guilt.** No "you failed", no streak-loss alarms. Even Fuming is a cartoon tantrum.
- **Pain and rehab always win.** Someone who logged pain gets the Patched up pose and the blue side for 72 h, never ember.
- **One way back, always short.** While Poulpy is ember, the catch-up offered is the shortest workout in the plan, and any finished session flips him straight back.
- **Nothing promised, nothing missed.** With no plan, Poulpy stays on the blue side and only reacts to when you last trained.
- **Poulpy never lifts his tentacles.** Every pose keeps the plush's shape and shows the mood with his face, shade, props and small effects. When he jumps, in 3D, they hang under him and follow.
- **At most one notification a day** in Poulpy's voice, and turning them off keeps the mascot.
- **Every line in French and English** from the start.
- **Inclusive by default.** The app is LGBTQ+ friendly, and that is part of the brand: see [Inclusive by default](?path=/docs/brand-inclusive-by-default--docs).


## Tentacles

Poulpy's ring has eight tentacles. Each finished session lights one, and **a lit tentacle never goes dark**: there is no weekly reset, so a quiet month takes nothing away. The eighth makes a **full octopus** (Champion), and the next session starts a new ring. This replaced "eight tentacles a week"; see [Brand decisions](?path=/docs/brand-decisions--docs), decision 11.

## The ember side

Only a plan can turn Poulpy ember, through its calendar entries. A planned workout placed by hand is a reminder, not a promise, and never counts. See [Brand decisions](?path=/docs/brand-decisions--docs), decision 12.

- **Annoyed (the grace period).** From 18:00 on an entry's day, while it is still planned, until the end of the next day. The entry can still be done the day after: finishing it then marks it done.
- **Grumpy.** An entry still planned once the day after its day is over: it is missed.
- **Fuming.** Two or more missed entries.
- **Any finished session flips him back.** Entries dated on or before the day of the last finished session never count, whatever their status. Finishing the catch-up, a free session or another entry all work.
- **Only planned entries count.** Done never counts; a moved entry counts on its new date only; a skipped entry never counts, because skipping is an honest choice and the ember side is for sessions left hanging.
- **Rest days never count.** A plan day with no workout has no entry. On a rest day Poulpy sleeps if he is blue and stays ember if he is ember: a rest day doesn't erase a missed session.
- **Pain and rehab win.** For 72 h after pain is logged or a rehab session is marked too hard, Poulpy is blue and Patched up, and entries dated inside those 72 h never count, even afterwards.
- **No enrollment, no ember.** Without an active enrollment the blue-side rules apply.

When several apply, the first wins: pain or rehab, then Fuming, Grumpy, Annoyed, then the blue-side rules.

**The catch-up** is offered with Grumpy and Fuming, and nowhere on the blue side. It is the workout with the shortest estimated duration in the enrolled plan version, the earliest in the plan on a tie. Finishing it flips Poulpy back like any session; it leaves the missed entries as they are, for the person to skip or move. While Annoyed there is no catch-up: today's entry is the way back.

# Inclusive by default

Poulpy is a plush with a blue side and a grumpy side, and nobody has to be one or the other. The app and everything around it welcomes LGBTQ+ people, and these rules apply to every screen, string, drawing and notification.

- **Ask only what a feature needs, and make every personal field optional.** Each field says why it is asked and where the answer is used. "Prefer not to say" is always an option and never a dead end: the app works the same without it.
- **Sex and gender are two questions, and the app asks the first only.** Where an estimate (calories, heart rate zones) depends on physiology, the field is called **Sex assigned at birth**, with Female, Male, Intersex and Prefer not to say, and a line saying it is not about gender. Avoid "biological sex", "birth sex" and "gender" for it. The app never asks about gender identity or orientation, and nothing it shows or says is decided by this field.
- **Pronouns are their own optional field**, a select (choosing Custom shows a text field beneath it): She / her, He / him, They / them, Just my name, a custom entry the person types, or Prefer not to say. They only set French agreement and Poulpy's lines. Without an answer, or with Prefer not to say, the wording stays neutral, a custom entry is shown as written but never drives French agreement, and a pronoun is never guessed from a name, a photo or the sex field.
- **Neutral wording first.** Write copy that needs no gender, in French too (« Bienvenue », « C'est fait », not « bienvenu(e) »). When agreement cannot be avoided, use the person's pronouns, and fall back to the neutral form.
- **Poulpy has no gender.** He is a plush; he is "he" only in English by habit and "il" in French because *poulpe* is masculine. Never write a line that depends on who the person is, and never joke about bodies.
- **No body judgement.** Weight, height and age are data. Poulpy never comments on a weight going up or down, charts use the one accent colour and never red or green for "worse" or "better", and the app shows no BMI and sets no weight goal unless the person asks.
- **Drawings and stories show a range of people.** Future share cards, the website and store images show different bodies, ages, genders and families, and may use the rainbow only as a deliberate Pride moment, never as decoration on Poulpy.
- **Review before shipping.** Wording about sex, gender or bodies is read by people from the community before release, and changed when they say so; the owner decides ties.

The reasons and the wording alternatives that were set aside are in [Brand decisions](?path=/docs/brand-decisions--docs#13-inclusive-by-default). The profile fields that follow these rules are on [Profile and settings](https://tkint.github.io/coach-poulpy/docs/profile-and-settings).

## The rules of the shape

These were settled with the owner on the first model; the reasons are in [Brand decisions](?path=/docs/brand-decisions--docs#14-poulpy-in-3d-a-bubble-plush-in-flat-shading).

- **Chibi, never realistic.** A big round head, big eyes, short arms. The face is the 2D drawing's, pressed onto the head, so every [drawing](?path=/docs/brand-drawings--docs) and the model show the same Poulpy.
- **The head is tucked in.** It sinks into the arms, which wrap around its base, instead of resting on top of them.
- **Eight arms, always down.** They lie on the floor as on the plush; a mood shows on his face and in his colour, never by lifting an arm. When he [jumps](?path=/docs/brand-poulpy-moves-little-jumps--docs) they hang under him.
- **The other side is underneath.** Each arm is one smooth shape, the side's colour on top and the other side's colour below a low line, with no ridge or shelf between them. The base of the head is cut at the same height, so the whole underside is one colour: ember under the blue side, blue under the ember side.
- **Flat, like a drawing.** Plain token colours, one soft shadow band and one faint gleam. No gloss, no reflections, no fabric texture.
- **The light belongs to the room.** It comes from the viewer's upper left and stays there: the shade and the gleam slide over Poulpy as he turns, they are never painted on him.
- **The outline is optional and off by default.** It is there for small sizes or busy backgrounds.

The model makes the six [moods](?path=/docs/brand-moods--docs) of the drawings: the same faces, pressed onto the head feature by feature, and the same shades, from the `mood` tokens. The side of the plush follows the mood, so the three ember moods show blue underneath.

- **The faces are the drawings'.** Each face in `model.mjs` is written in the 2D drawing's own coordinates, so the two can be read side by side and a change to one is copied to the other.
- **A mood changes his face and his shade, never his shape.** Heavy lids, brows, a smirk or clenched teeth lie on the head like the eyes do; his arms stay down.


## His twelve poses

The model also takes the twelve [poses](?path=/docs/brand-moods--docs) of the drawings, each in the shade of its mood. They are in [`design/poulpy-3d/poses.mjs`](https://github.com/tkint/coach-poulpy/blob/main/design/poulpy-3d/poses.mjs), built on the shape and the faces of `model.mjs`.

- **What he wears is solid.** The sweatband goes all the way around his head, the nightcap sits on it, the medal rests on his front arm and the dumbbell lies on the floor beside him: turn him and they turn with him.
- **What is on him is on his skin.** The plasters and the vein are laid against his head where the drawing has them and wrapped around it; sweat and tears are part of his face. Turn him and they stay where they are.
- **The comic effects stay flat and face the viewer.** Sparks, confetti, puffs, bubbles and lettering are the drawing's own, standing in front of him or behind him, and they keep facing whoever looks while he turns.
- **Cocky has no halo and no "PR!" badge in 3D.** The owner dropped them; his sparks stay, and become solid stars when he [winks](?path=/docs/brand-poulpy-moves-the-cocky-wink--docs).
- **He still never lifts an arm.** Standing tall stretches his arms taller and lifts his head with them; Side glance leans his head. Nothing else moves.
- **The loader is his outline from underneath, drawn frame by frame, with loose tentacles.** It is made from the model itself, seen from straight below, with his tentacles wider and shorter than in the other pictures so that neighbours touch at their base. Only the edge of his shape is drawn, in the brand blue and see-through, as the old loader was, rounded, with no line inside. Each tentacle is loose: it bends on the same arm springs as his turn (`createFollow` in `motion.mjs`) and trails behind the turn. A cycle is 48 frames in one sheet, showing how the tentacles hang at that moment relative to his body; the apps and the website play them in turn and turn the whole thing smoothly. He swings half a turn one way and then half a turn the other (from -180° to 180°), forever, once every 3.6 s (once every 12 s with reduced motion), easing at each end. The other version only turns clockwise, a full turn every 3.6 s, with his tentacles trailing the same all the time, so it is one picture (`clockwise` on the phones, `spin="clockwise"` on the web). Which frame and how far it is turned is `LoaderSwing` in the shared Kotlin, and `PoulpyLoader` on Android and iOS, `MobilePoulpyLoader` and `CpPoulpyLoader` in the workshop all follow it.

| [`design/poulpy-3d/motion.mjs`](https://github.com/tkint/coach-poulpy/blob/main/design/poulpy-3d/motion.mjs) | How he moves, the same way |

The glTF files open in any 3D tool or engine. Poulpy stands on the floor (y = 0, y up) and faces +z; his head is about one unit from centre to edge. In the two files that stand still, each part is its own named mesh (`head`, `head_lining`, `arm_1` to `arm_8` and their `_lining`, `eye_left`, `eye_left_shine`, `cheek_left`, `mouth`, `gleam`, plus `brow_left` and `brow_right` on the ember side; left and right are Poulpy's own), so a part can be recoloured, hidden or animated on its own.

The two `_turn` files hold the same meshes, rigged. The node `turn` faces him and `squash` under it carries his head and face; each arm is skinned to `turn` and to its own chain of three joints, `arm_1_bend_1` to `arm_1_bend_3` from the head outwards. The animation `turn` plays one full turn at 30 frames a second and ends where it starts, so any player can loop it. It is a recording of the springs at their design values: an engine that wants them live, to change the softness or to react to something, runs `createTurn()` from `motion.mjs` on the same joints, as the workshop's preview does.

A glTF file only carries shapes and plain matte colours. The look is the renderer's job, and `LOOK` in `model.mjs` gives its numbers: the light's direction as the viewer sees it, the shadow band's strength, where the gleam sits, and the outline's width and tint. A renderer that wants the gleam to follow the light drops the `gleam` mesh from the file and asks `gleamPatch(direction)` for a new one whenever Poulpy or the camera turns. [`poulpy3dScene.ts`](https://github.com/tkint/coach-poulpy/blob/main/web/storybook/stories/brand/poulpy3dScene.ts), which draws the workshop's preview with three.js, is the worked example.


## Changing the design

Everything that defines him is at the top of `model.mjs`, in three plainly named objects:

- `SHAPE`: the size of the head and how deep it sits, the number, size and reach of the arms, the height of the line between the two colours, and where the face sits.
- `FACES`: the eyes, lids, brows, mouth and cheeks of each face, feature by feature, in the coordinates of the 2D drawings of `design/build-poulpy.mjs`.
- `MOODS`: for each of his six moods, the side of the plush and the face he makes. `buildPoulpy3d({ mood, tone })` builds him in one, tinted with that mood's `mood` token.
- `LOOK`: the light, the shadow band, the gleam and the outline.

His poses are in `poses.mjs`: `POSES` gives each one its mood, its face, what he wears, what lies on his head and the effects around him, all in the drawing's coordinates, and `buildPoulpyPose({ pose })` builds it.

How he moves is in `motion.mjs`: `TURN` for his turn, `JUMP` for his jump, `CHEER` for the champion's, `WINK` for the cocky one's and `RIG` for where the arms bend.

His colours are the `color.poulpy` tokens of [`design/tokens.json`](https://github.com/tkint/coach-poulpy/blob/main/design/tokens.json), never written into the model.

Edit, then regenerate and look at the workshop's page (`npm run storybook` in `web/`):

```sh
node design/build-poulpy-3d.mjs
```

CI runs it with `--check` and fails when the committed glTF files no longer match `model.mjs` and `motion.mjs`. A new mood in 3D starts as a new entry in `FACES` and `MOODS`, a new pose as a new entry in `POSES`; a new move starts next to `TURN`, `JUMP`, `CHEER` and `WINK` in `motion.mjs`, with a page of its own in the workshop. Both follow the [brand rules](?path=/docs/brand-character--docs) like any drawing.

# Turning in short steps

His idle move: Poulpy likes to turn around on the spot, a short step of 20 to 30 degrees at a time, stopping after each one. Fifteen steps bring him all the way around, in about 17 seconds, and then he goes again.

<Story of={Live.Turning} />

His arms are not stiff. Each one bends at three places, and every bend is a small spring: when he steps the arms trail behind, when he stops they swing a little past him and settle, the tips last. The controls of the turning model are there to try other feels, and each shows its value so a setting can be quoted and written into the design. **Step** sets how far he turns each time, from 1 to 180 degrees, keeping the small differences between one step and the next; 24 is the design. **Soft arms** loosens or stiffens those springs: 1 is the design, 0 is the stiff plush with no springs at all and 4 is very floppy. **Speed** plays the whole move faster or slower, 1 being the design. The two arrows pick the direction, seen from above; the design turns anticlockwise, to his left.

- **The arms slide, they never lift.** Every bend turns flat on the floor, so the move keeps the plush's shape as the [brand rules](?path=/docs/brand-character--docs) ask.
- **He winds up, then goes.** A small lean back before each step, a quick turn, and a slight rock past the mark as he stops.
- **The head dips as he turns** and bounces back, by a few hundredths of its height, widening to keep its volume. His face rides along with it.
- **No two steps or arms are quite alike.** The steps vary between 20 and 30 degrees and the pauses between 0.45 and 0.75 seconds, and each arm's springs are a little stiffer or softer than its neighbours', so the arms fall out of step with each other.
- **Reduced motion keeps him still.** The preview starts paused for anyone who asked their device for less motion, and an app must do the same.

`TURN` at the top of `motion.mjs` holds every number: the steps and pauses, the wind-up, how eager and how bouncy his body is, how soft each length of arm is, how much the floor holds the arms back and how far the head dips. `createTurn()` runs it live, as the workshop's page does, and `bakeTurn()` writes it down frame by frame for the glTF files.

# Brand decisions

What was decided about Poulpy and the brand, why, and when. The [brand page](?path=/docs/brand-introduction--docs) and the brand book say what Poulpy is today; this page keeps the reasons, so a later change can see what it overturns. Architecture decisions live in [`docs/adr/`](https://tkint.github.io/coach-poulpy/decisions/) instead.

Add a decision here in the same PR as the brand change it explains, newest at the bottom. When a decision is overturned, keep it, mark it superseded and link the one that replaces it.

## 1. Logo A: the plush as he is

**Accepted, 24 October 2026.** The logo is a faithful front view of the plush: tall dome, five visible arms and the ember lining peeking out underneath. The logo and the in-app mascot are the same drawing.

**Why:** it is Poulpy himself, the one real plush the app is built around, and one drawing for both keeps him recognisable everywhere.

**Set aside:** B, Poulpy split down the middle with one happy and one grumpy half; C, a letter P made from his head and a curled arm.

## 2. Baloo 2 for headings, Nunito for text

**Accepted, 24 October 2026.** Baloo 2 for headings, Poulpy's speech and big moments; Nunito for body text, labels and numbers.

**Why:** both are round like the plush, and both are free (SIL Open Font License), so they ship inside the apps and load from Google Fonts on the web. Nunito has tabular figures, so timers and rep counts don't jitter while they tick.

## 3. The grumpy side is ember, not pink

**Accepted, 24 October 2026.** The real plush's grumpy side is pink; in the brand it is ember, from burnt orange to red, and the lining under the blue side follows it. Errors move to a crimson (`#C8264A`) and always carry an icon and text.

**Why:** the owner wanted a grumpy side that reads redder and less pink. Crimson errors with an icon keep a cross Poulpy from ever being mistaken for an error.

## 4. Burnt orange is the ember

**Accepted, 24 October 2026.** The everyday grumpy shade is burnt orange, `#D9622B`. Buttons use the deeper ember 600 (`#B4471C`), because the plush ember is too light for white text.

**Why:** warm, playful and clearly not an error, and it has room to deepen into red for Fuming.

**Set aside:** brick (`#C2452D`), moodier and with less room to go redder; vermilion (`#E4572E`), loud and the closest to a warning colour.

## 5. The side is the status, the shade is the feeling

**Accepted, 24 October 2026.** Which side shows says whether you are keeping your plan; the shade on that side moves with what just happened.

**Why:** the owner asked for shades that change with accomplishments and emotions, and real octopuses change colour with their mood.

## 6. Six moods, three blues, no grey

**Accepted, 24 October 2026.** The spectrum is Proud, Happy and Recovering on the blue side; Annoyed, Grumpy and Fuming on the ember side. Tired, Calm and rest merged into one light blue, Recovering. Proud sits just next to Happy.

**Why:** the first spectrum's grey Tired felt uneasy, even sick, and there were too many blues. Proud read too far from Poulpy's own colour.

## 7. No hearts; a grace period instead

**Accepted, 24 October 2026.** The "three hearts a month" idea, which let you miss sessions without Poulpy turning grumpy, is dropped. Instead a session still due by the evening only makes Poulpy Annoyed, and he turns Grumpy 24 hours after its day.

**Why:** the owner kept every game idea except the hearts. The grace period gives the same slack without something to count down.

## 8. Poulpy never lifts his tentacles

**Accepted, 24 October 2026.** Every pose keeps the plush's shape and shows the mood with his face, shade, props and small effects. The poses that needed arms were renamed for what they show: Flexing became Cocky, Lifting became Straining, Hiding his eyes became Shy, Head scratch became Thinking and Arms crossed became Pouting.

**Why:** Poulpy has eight tentacles and no arms, so the first poses with added arms were wrong. Lifting his own tentacles was tried next, first long, then cut to the plush's real length, and still didn't look like him.

## 9. Poses follow the real plush

**Accepted, 24 October 2026.** The pose sheet stays close to how the plush actually sits and moves:

- Standing tall, Side glance, Shy, Thinking and Spinning come from photos of the plush.
- Standing tall is the usual plush with the base of his tentacles slightly stretched, with no gaps.
- Spinning, the loading indicator, is drawn comic-style with motion lines, and only the front arc moves.
- Champion has no arms, Sleeping has a short upright nightcap and no bed, Patched up has no bubble, and Exhausted has no puddle.

**Why:** each was a correction from the owner. The first Standing tall showed gaps around his body, and the first Spinning flattened him like a card instead of turning him.

## 10. Colours generated from one tokens file

**Accepted, 24 October 2026.** Once the colours were validated, they went into `design/tokens.json` and are generated into each platform's theme. See [ADR 0012](https://tkint.github.io/coach-poulpy/docs/adr/0012-design-tokens-generated-from-one-file).

## 11. Tentacles never reset

**Accepted, 24 October 2026.** Each finished session lights one of the eight tentacles, and a lit tentacle never goes dark. The eighth makes a full octopus and the next session starts a new ring. This replaces "eight tentacles a week", where the ring emptied every Monday.

**Why:** eight sessions a week is a lot, and a weekly reset punishes the people who train least. A ring that only fills keeps Poulpy positive for them.

## 12. The ember-side rules

**Accepted, 26 October 2026**, for [#17](https://github.com/tkint/coach-poulpy/issues/17). The [brand page](?path=/docs/brand-moods--docs) sets when Poulpy turns ember. Annoyed runs from 18:00 on an entry's day to the end of the next day; the entry is missed after that, which makes him Grumpy, and two missed entries make him Fuming. Only plan calendar entries count, never planned workouts placed by hand. Skipped entries never count. Any finished session forgives every entry dated on or before its day. Entries inside a 72 h pain or rehab window never count. The catch-up is the plan's shortest workout and leaves missed entries as they are.

**Why:** decision 7 promised a grace period "until 24 hours after its day", and reading it as the whole next day gives a person who trains in the morning a real second chance. Counting only what is still planned keeps the ember side for sessions left hanging, not for honest choices: a skip, a move, a rest day or pain. Forgiving by date rather than by matching each entry keeps "any finished session flips him straight back" literally true.

**Set aside:** a skip counting as missed, which makes skipping feel like a confession; the catch-up marking a missed entry done, which rewrites History.

## 13. Inclusive by default

**Accepted, 1 November 2026.** The owner wants the app to be LGBTQ+ friendly as part of the brand. The [brand page](?path=/docs/brand-inclusive-by-default--docs) turns that into rules: optional fields with a stated reason, **Sex assigned at birth** asked only where an estimate needs it, pronouns as a separate field, neutral wording, no body judgement.

**Why:** "sex assigned at birth" is the wording most inclusive-forms guides recommend for a physiological question, because it describes what was recorded rather than who someone is, and it keeps gender out of it; "biological sex" and "birth sex" read as defining a person and are the terms trans and intersex people most often object to. The app also needs no gender at all: sex assigned at birth drives estimates, and pronouns drive wording.

**Set aside:** one "Gender" field with Male, Female and Other (which mixes the two questions and makes "Other" the label for everyone outside two boxes); free-text sex (cannot feed an estimate); asking nothing (the estimates become less accurate for people who are happy to say). The wording was chosen from published guides and has **not yet been read by LGBTQ+ people**: that read is still to do, and the wording changes if it asks for it.

## 14. Poulpy in 3D: a bubble plush in flat shading

**Accepted, 14 November 2026.** Poulpy's 3D form is a round head tucked into eight puffy arms, drawn with flat cartoon shading. The rules of the shape are on the [Poulpy in 3D](?path=/docs/brand-poulpy-in-3d--docs) page, and the shape is one file, [`design/poulpy-3d/model.mjs`](https://github.com/tkint/coach-poulpy/blob/main/design/poulpy-3d/model.mjs), so it can be reused and improved like the 2D drawings.

**Why:** the owner wanted him chibi, natural and simple, "a bit like the mascot of Duolingo", so the model had to read like the 2D drawings turned into a solid, not like a rendered toy. Each rule was a correction from the owner on a model he could turn: the head sinks into the arms because resting on top of them looked stacked; the two colours share one smooth surface because a separate lining made a shelf; the whole underside is the other side's colour because a patch of the top colour showed between the arms; and the light stays in the room because shade painted on the model turned with him.

**Set aside:** a dome head on puffy arms, the 2D drawing extruded (the head he preferred was rounder); a glossy vinyl ball on tiny feet (too photorealistic, arms too short); an egg head on long curling arms (less chibi, and too close to a real octopus); a one-piece bell with a scalloped hem. A dark outline is kept as an option, off by default.

## 15. Poulpy jumps, and his tentacles hang

**Accepted, 15 November 2026.** In 3D Poulpy can do little jumps, on the spot or turning a step at each. He tucks his head into his tentacles, which widen flat on the floor, then goes up; his tentacles stay joined to his head and their tips hang a little lower, barely off the floor. He tilts to one side in the air, and his head goes a little down into his tentacles as he lands. The move and its controls are on the [Poulpy in 3D](?path=/docs/brand-poulpy-moves-little-jumps--docs) page.

This narrows [decision 8](?path=/docs/brand-decisions--docs#8-poulpy-never-lifts-his-tentacles) without overturning it: Poulpy still never raises a tentacle himself, as an arm would. In a jump they leave the floor only because he does, hanging soft under him, tips last.

**Why:** the owner asked for the jump and described it. The first preview let his head come up out of his tentacles; he corrected it: the head is always joined to them, and they may hang much steeper instead. Turning, one jump may be a whole turn, or two. It is kept small so he stays the plush: a hop that barely clears the floor, not a leap.

**Still open:** the jump's values (how high, how deep the tuck and the landing, how far the tentacles widen and hang, how much tilt, how long between jumps). It runs as a live preview with a control for each, and is written into the glTF files once the owner has settled them.

## 16. The 3D Poulpy replaces the drawings

**Accepted, 15 November 2026.** The 2D drawings (six mood portraits, twelve poses, the loader and the app icons) are to be replaced by the 3D Poulpy. Everywhere a drawing shows today, a picture rendered from the model takes its place, generated like the drawings are; on the key screens, Home and the end of a session, he is live and animated instead.

It is done in steps, each reviewed on the [Poulpy in 3D](?path=/docs/brand-poulpy-in-3d--docs) page before the apps change:

1. The six moods on the model: faces and shades. Done with this decision.
2. The twelve poses and their props (done), then the loader (done: his outline from underneath, frame by frame).
3. Pictures rendered from the model, generated for Android, iOS and the website in place of the drawings. The pictures are generated and shown on the page, and the website, the docs and both apps use them: Android takes PNGs under the same `poulpy_<name>` names, iOS takes 3x imagesets under the same asset names.
\1 On Home he is not animated all the time: he stays a still picture, and a tap plays one short animation for his current mood, then he settles back. Happy and proud jump for now; the other moods get theirs later, and until then a tap does nothing. With reduced motion a tap does nothing either. The Home mockup with the tap is agreed (`Mobile/Screens/Home`, Planned, tap Poulpy): each tap plays one of two jumps at random, leaning to his left or to his right, and happy Poulpy sometimes does a high jump with a full turn on himself (the spin, tuned by the owner). The phones have no 3D engine for this: the jumps are baked from the model, frame by frame, with the same shot as his still picture (30 frames at 24 a second, 1.25 s), and an engine comes only if he is made interactive later. The champion's tap on Home is baked the same way (72 frames at 20 a second, in three sheets, ending on his still picture), and on the finished screen he never stops: a seamless 4.8 s loop of four jumps (96 frames, four sheets) plays over and over for as long as the screen is open.

The app icons are his pictures on Deep sea, in light and dark alike, and the loader is his outline seen from underneath with wider, shorter, loose tentacles, drawn frame by frame from the 3D model, swinging half a turn one way and then half a turn the other (or only turning clockwise). `design/build-poulpy.mjs` stays the source of the one-colour layer of the Android themed icons.

**Why:** the owner asked for it once the model could turn and jump, and chose pictures everywhere with live 3D on key screens over a 3D renderer on every screen, which would weigh on the apps for little gain on small portraits.

**Settled:** the pictures are painted by our own script in plain JavaScript (`design/poulpy-3d/picture.mjs`), so every machine writes the same pixels and CI can check them, as for the app icons. The owner chose it over photographing the workshop in a browser, whose pixels differ from machine to machine, and over tracing vector pictures, which would take far longer and flatten the shading.

## 17. In 3D, what is on Poulpy stays on him, the champion cheers and the cocky one winks

**Accepted, 15 November 2026.** Two rules for the 3D poses, and two moves:

- Sweat and tears are part of his face, and plasters or a vein are wrapped onto his head. They stay on his skin when he turns. Only what is around him (sparks, puffs, bubbles, lettering) is flat and faces the viewer.
- The champion has a move: he jumps for joy, his medal swings on its ribbon as a weight would and confetti flies at each jump, then lies on the floor. It is on [its own page](?path=/docs/brand-poulpy-moves-the-champion--docs).
- The cocky one has a move: he starts with both eyes wide open, then winks with his slight cocky smile. His stars appear with the wink, solid and twinkling. In 3D he has no halo and no "PR!" badge. It is on [its own page](?path=/docs/brand-poulpy-moves-the-cocky-wink--docs).
- Each move has a page of its own in the component workshop, and a preview only moves while it is on screen.

**Why:** the owner asked for each on seeing the twelve poses in 3D. Drops that faced the viewer slid over him as he turned, where sweat should stick; the plasters were pressed on straight from the front and dipped under his skin near the edge of his head; and several moves running on one page made it lag. For the cocky one he asked for the wink, for stars that move in 3D instead of a still drawing, and to drop the badge and the halo.

**Still open:** the cheer's and the wink's values, like the jump's. They are live previews, not in the glTF files yet.
