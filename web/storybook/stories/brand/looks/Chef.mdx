# Poulpy the cook

**Draft.** Poulpy dressed up as a cook who is sure of everything: a tall white toque worn a little askew, a big curled French moustache and a confident grin.

<Story of={Live.Chef} />

- **His face** is a small, confident toothy grin, short from top to bottom, its top tucked under the moustache so only a few teeth show. His eyes are wide open and both brows are up, set high so the moustache has room. It is our own drawing, made for Poulpy.
- **The moustache is solid and big.** A thick, wide handlebar, a rounded volume that stands out from his face like the toque does, tapering from his middle to two tips that curl up beside his eyes, never over them. It is shaded like him, in a dark a little lighter than his ink, belongs to his face (it goes with his head and twitches) and lies over the grin's top teeth.
- **The toque is solid.** A plain band, then a puff with soft folds and a light crease in each. It sits on his head, leans 9° to his left and 5° back, and goes wherever his head goes.
- **His idle is small.** He stands still, then puffs himself up: his chin goes up 6° and his head grows a little taller, on a spring, and while he holds it his moustache twitches twice, tips up. Then he settles back. `CHEF` in `motion.mjs` holds the timing.
- **Still Poulpy.** Proud, on the blue side, eight tentacles on the floor and no arms (brand decision 8). Turn him with the slider: the toque and the moustache stay where they belong.

The look is in [`design/poulpy-3d/looks/chef.mjs`](https://github.com/tkint/coach-poulpy/blob/main/design/poulpy-3d/looks/chef.mjs): `TOQUE`, `MOUSTACHE`, `SMILE` and `CHEF_FACE`. It is a preview only: not in the glTF files, the pictures or the apps.
