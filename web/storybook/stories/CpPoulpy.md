`CpPoulpy` shows one of Poulpy's pictures, decorative unless it has a `label`; `size` is its width in pixels. His moods and poses are painted from the 3D model by `design/build-poulpy-pictures.mjs`, and the loader, Poulpy from above, transparent so they sit on a light or a dark page: see Core, Poulpy for all of them, and `PoulpyDrawing` for the names the `drawing` prop takes.

`CpPoulpyCard` is the card for empty lists, no results and gone pages: see Web, Patterns, Poulpy card.

| Vue | Classes |
| --- | --- |
| `<CpPoulpy>` | `img.cp-poulpy` from `design/poulpy-3d/pictures/` |
| `<CpPoulpyCard>` | `cp-card bg-secondary text-secondary-content cp-poulpy-card` |
