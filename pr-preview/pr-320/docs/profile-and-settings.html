Profile and settings ​

The profile, body logging with charts, and a settings hub that reaches the Devices view. The screens are agreed (see ADR 0016) and are built from the pages under Mockups, Agreed, in the site menu. The wording rules behind it are in Inclusive by default.

Settings, reworked ​

Settings stops being two tabs (General, Devices) and becomes a hub, because it now holds more than two things and Devices deserves a row, not a tab.

DraftWhat it shows
settings-generalThe hub: a profile card (Poulpy, name, age, height, weight), then Body (Weight, Height), App (Appearance and units, Devices) and About
profile-editThe profile fields below, in two groups, About you and Training
profile-sex-sheetThe sex assigned at birth picker, with its reason
profile-body-figure-sheetThe body figure picker: Wider shoulders or Wider hips
profile-pain-sheetPain or rehab: the front and back body map, then for how long

body-log-sheetLogging a weight: a stepper in the person's units and a date
settings-preferencesTheme, Language and Units as selects, moved out of the old General tab
devices-listThe existing Devices view with a back bar instead of a tab; its detail, add and data source screens stay as agreed

They replace the old settings-general and devices-list.

Why "sex assigned at birth" ​

The only thing the app needs is a physiological input for estimates, so that is all it asks, and it names it as what it is: assigned at birth, not "biological" or "birth" sex, and not gender. Intersex is offered and Prefer not to say is always available. Gender is never asked, because no feature needs it. See decision 13 for what was set aside. This wording comes from published inclusive-forms guidance and has not yet been read by LGBTQ+ people; that read is still to do and does not block building.

Weight and height logging ​

  • Each entry is a date and a value, added from the Log button and corrected or deleted from the history list. One entry per day; a second one that day replaces it.
  • Charts are lines with a dot per entry on the one accent colour. The ranges are 1M, 3M, 1Y and All. Three tiles under the weight chart give the change over the range, the lowest and the highest.
  • Height changes slowly, so its screen says a measure every few months is plenty, and teenagers can log as often as they like.
  • A weight going up is never red and going down is never green. Poulpy says nothing about it, there is no BMI and no weight goal unless a later issue adds one the person opts into.
  • A line chart is a new component, line-chart, drawn in the mockup with an SVG. Every chart has a text alternative naming its first and last value.

Units ​

The unit system chosen in Settings decides how weight (kg or lb), height (cm or ft and in) and distance (km or mi) are shown and typed. Everything is stored metric, so switching converts and never rewrites; see ADR 0017. The weight pages are drawn Metric and the height page Imperial so both can be reviewed.

Pain or rehab ​

The profile row opens a sheet. The screens are profile-edit (the row filled in, with Poulpy's note) and profile-pain-sheet (a body map where it hurts, one side at a time with a Front and Back switch so every area is big enough to tap, left and right apart, then for how long: 72 hours by default, 1 week, 2 weeks or until the person clears it). The body map is a new component, body-map, meant to be reused later to show the areas an exercise works. It is a muscle chart with finer regions (pectoral, biceps, quadriceps, hamstring...) and no fill on skin, hair or face, so it implies no skin tone. It has two builds, picked in profile-body-figure-sheet, under Mockups, Agreed. Duration is 72 hours by default, with 1 week, 2 weeks or until the person clears it; the window only changes Poulpy (blue side, Patched up pose, no missed entries) and never filters workouts. The rules they follow are in Character rules: pain or rehab always shows the blue side and the Patched up pose, never ember, and entries inside the window never count as missed (decision 8).
