# Rodolphe, the red-nosed reindeer

**Draft.** A festive look to review here before it goes anywhere else: Poulpy dresses up as a red-nosed reindeer, our own take on the old idea and no one else's character, and he is very happy about it.

<Story of={Live.Rodolphe} />

- **Always the blue side, always happy.** Rodolphe is a treat, so he never wears the ember side and never frowns.
- **A felt reindeer headband.** A flat, broad strip goes over the top of his head, leaning a little back. Seen from above it is wide front to back; seen from the front it is a solid felt band, and it stops well above his sides. Two big, tall, chunky dark-brown felt antlers sit low on it, close to his head. Each is a long fat beam that rises well above his head and bows out, with two round tines branching outwards and a little gold jingle bell on every tip. Below and outside each antler is a felt reindeer ear, tan outside and cream inside, pointing outwards. At the front of each antler's base is a full cluster of holly: six round-ended green leaves fanned out, none pointing down towards his eyes, and five red berries in the middle. It is all soft and plush, with no points, and the design is our own.
- **A round, shiny red nose.** A bulb half out of his face, between his eyes and his smile, with a white dot of light like his eyes have. His happy face moves up a little to make room for it and his smile is a little smaller.
- **A white and red striped scarf.** Puffy knitted pieces all around the base of his head, sitting on his arms: lower in front, under his smile, higher behind. It is knotted over his front right arm and its two ends lie along that arm. Turn the view and everything he wears goes round with him.
- **His reds are not an alarm.** The nose and the scarf are a bright, warm cherry (`#F2353A`, `#E2373F`), lighter and yellower than the error crimson (`#C8264A`) and redder than any ember shade, and they only ever show on blue. Nothing about him reads as an error or as anger. The berries are the scarf's red. The antlers are `#5E3A22`, the headband `#9A6A42`, the bells the medal's gold `#F0B53A`, the ears `#C89A6A` with a `#F5E6CC` inside and the holly `#2E8B4E`; the nose lights up towards `#FF8C8C` and its glow is `#FF5A4E`, fading out.
- **Flat, like the rest of him.** One shadow band, the plain colours below, no gloss: the nose's shine is a white dot, as on his eyes.
- **He sways to a tune.** A step to his left, a step to his right: his body turns a little each way on a spring and his arms trail behind it as they do when he turns, his head leans into each step and bobs on the beat, and the scarf's ends swing behind. Every eight beats he holds still for two, his nose still glowing.
- **His nose glows on the beat.** Every other beat it lights up, swells a hair and a warm glow blooms in front of it, then it fades back. The glow is light, so it belongs to whoever draws him, like the gleam; the model only holds the nose.
- **No arms.** His eight arms stay on the floor; the dance is in his body, his head and the scarf. The scarf's ends lie on an arm, they are not held.

[`design/poulpy-3d/looks/rodolphe.mjs`](https://github.com/tkint/coach-poulpy/blob/main/design/poulpy-3d/looks/rodolphe.mjs) holds the whole look, built like a pose and moving like a move: `FACE` and `COLOURS`, the shapes in `buildRodolphe()`, and `IDLE`, the dance's timing and springs, which `createRodolpheIdle()` runs. `PoulpyRodolphe.vue` beside this page adds the glow. It is a preview only: not in the glTF files, the pictures or the apps.

**Still open:** whether he keeps the name Rodolphe; when he would show (a December look, a reward, a choice in settings); whether the dance is his idle or a move he does when tapped; and whether he goes into the glTF files and the pictures once agreed.
