Mockups ​

The screen mockups from design/mockups/, as they are on main (ADR 0016). Each app view has its own page, with its available states shown together. This keeps related states easy to compare without mixing separate areas of the app.

Mockups are split by platform, then by stage:

  • Mobile: the Android and iOS apps (phone and tablet screens), drawn with design/mockups/shared/mockup.css.
    • Agreed: screens the owner validated. The apps are built from these.
    • Drafts: screens being explored or redesigned. A draft becomes agreed through a pull request that moves it out of drafts/.
  • Web: the websites, drawn with the web components. Each view (for example, Admin · Catalog or Admin · Accounts) has its own page.
