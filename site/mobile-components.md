# Mobile components

The Android and iOS components, drawn with the values of [`design/tokens.json`](design/tokens.json). The shape and notes of each one are in the [Components table](/docs/design-system#components) of the design system; the fields every form is built from are in its [Base fields table](/docs/design-system#base-fields), and each entry links to its sample here. Use the switch to see the samples in light or dark; it is remembered apart from the site's own theme.
