# Poulpy the cook

**Draft.** Poulpy dressed up as a cook who is sure of everything: a tall white toque worn a little askew, a big curled French moustache and a confident grin.

<Story of={Live.Chef} />

- **His face** is a confident grin, narrower than his cheeks, with a few top teeth showing under the moustache and the bottom row under them. His eyes are wide open and both brows are up, set high so the moustache has room. It is our own drawing, made for Poulpy.
- **The moustache is big.** A wide handlebar in his ink, thick under his middle and curling up at both tips beside his eyes, never over them. It is pressed onto his head like his eyes and brows, so it stays on his skin when he turns, and it lies over the grin's top teeth.
- **The toque is solid.** A plain band, then a puff with soft folds and a light crease in each. It sits on his head, leans 9° to his left and 5° back, and goes wherever his head goes.
- **His idle is small.** He stands still, then puffs himself up: his chin goes up 6° and his head grows a little taller, on a spring, and while he holds it his moustache twitches twice, tips up. Then he settles back. `CHEF` in `motion.mjs` holds the timing.
- **Still Poulpy.** Proud, on the blue side, eight tentacles on the floor and no arms (brand decision 8). Turn him with the slider: the toque and the moustache stay where they belong.

The look is in [`design/poulpy-3d/looks/chef.mjs`](https://github.com/tkint/coach-poulpy/blob/main/design/poulpy-3d/looks/chef.mjs): `TOQUE`, `MOUSTACHE` and `CHEF_FACE`. It is a preview only: not in the glTF files, the pictures or the apps.
