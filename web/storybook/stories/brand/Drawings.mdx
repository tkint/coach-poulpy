# Drawings

Poulpy's six mood portraits, his poses and the loader. The portraits and poses are pictures painted from the [3D model](?path=/docs/brand-poulpy-in-3d--docs) by [`design/build-poulpy-pictures.mjs`](https://github.com/tkint/coach-poulpy/blob/main/design/build-poulpy-pictures.mjs), transparent so they sit on a light or a dark screen, and so is the loader, Poulpy seen from above. Portraits and poses share one 248 × 226 frame, so they can replace each other without moving. When each one shows is on the [Moods](?path=/docs/brand-moods--docs#when-each-drawing-shows) page. They are all under [Core > Poulpy](?path=/docs/core-poulpy--docs), as the website uses them.
