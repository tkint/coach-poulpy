# Poulpy in autumn

**Draft.** A look for the season, to review here before it goes anywhere else: Poulpy, happy and cosy in a little white and red striped scarf, while maple leaves fall around him.

<Story of={Live.Autumn} />

- **He is happy Poulpy, on the blue side.** A look is never a mood of its own: it is his happy face and shade, with cheeks a little rosier from the cold.
- **The scarf is part of him.** It goes all the way around his head where his arms meet it, sinks a little into their soft tops and is knotted on his left, above his second arm. Its two ends lie over that arm and hang off its tip, with a short fringe. Turn him and it turns with him; it never covers his smile.
- **The scarf's red is poppy, not the error crimson nor the ember side.** Off-white wool (`#FBF6EE`) and poppy red (`#E5373A`), brighter and warmer than the crimson of errors (`#C8264A`) and redder than the ember (`#D9622B`). With its white stripes it reads as knitting, never as a state.
- **The leaves are flat, like the comic effects of his poses.** Three autumn oranges, each a five-lobed maple leaf. Falling, a leaf faces the viewer and swings from side to side like a pendulum: it falls quickest through the middle of a swing and tilts up at each end, and now and then half turns over. On the floor it settles flat, stays a moment and goes, and another one falls from the top. They fall around him, never through him.
- **He sways and breathes.** Once every 6.4 s he turns a little to one side and the other, his head leaning a moment later, and his head stretches and settles with his breath. His arms trail the sway on the same springs as his turn, and they stay on the floor.
- **Every run is the same.** Where each leaf falls and how it swings comes from a fixed list, not from chance, and the leaves have already been falling for a while when he appears.

`AUTUMN` in [`design/poulpy-3d/looks/autumn.mjs`](https://github.com/tkint/coach-poulpy/blob/main/design/poulpy-3d/looks/autumn.mjs) holds every number: his cheeks, the scarf, the leaves and his idle. `buildAutumn()` builds him in his scarf and `createAutumn()` runs the leaves and the sway live. Like the moves, it is a preview: it is not in the glTF files nor in the apps' pictures.

**Still open:** its values (the scarf's colours, how many leaves fall and how fast, how far he sways).
