Poulpy is a reversible plush octopus: blue and smiling on one side, ember and grumpy on the other. The side is the status and the shade is the feeling. Every drawing here is generated by `design/build-poulpy.mjs` into `design/poulpy/` and shared by every platform: the apps draw the same files with their own code.

A **mood** is Poulpy's face and shade for what just happened (Proud, Happy, Recovering are blue; Annoyed, Grumpy, Fuming are ember). A **pose** shows an activity and keeps whatever mood he is in. Pain and rehab always show the blue side; Poulpy is never guilt. The rules for when each drawing shows are in the Brand book; the web component that draws them is under Web, Components, Display, Poulpy.
