# Components

| Component | Shape | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Top bar](?path=/docs/mobile-components-top-bar--docs) | One bar on every phone screen: 44 under the status bar, then a 44 row. The title is a headline (21) on the left; a back chevron (pushed screen) or a close cross (screen that ends a task) before it, none on a tab's root; at most two actions after it. Tabs, search and filters sit below the bar, never in it | `MobileTopBar` in the mockups. Replaces the screen header, Live title bar, History head, Settings head and back bar |
| [Motivation row](?path=/docs/mobile-components-motivation-row--docs) | Home's one motivation row: a pill with a small Poulpy, eight segments (the tentacles, lit in `primary`), the latest achievements and a chevron | `MobileMotivationRow`. A tap opens Achievements. No streak: a lit tentacle never goes dark |
| [Achievement](?path=/docs/mobile-components-achievement--docs) | A round token: earned in `primaryContainer` with a Lucide icon, or locked as a quiet "?" in `surfaceVariant`; 28 in a row, 64 on Achievements | `MobileAchievement`. Locked ones never say what they ask for |
| [Button](?path=/docs/mobile-components-button--docs) | Rounded square (10 radius), never a pill, on the websites' size scale: xs 24, sm 32, md 40 (the default), lg 48, xl 56, xxl 64; below 44 the touch area still reaches 44 | `MobileButton` in the mockups, `PoulpyButton` on both platforms (`PoulpyButtonKind`, `PoulpyButtonSize`). Primary: one per screen. Tonal (`primaryContainer`): secondary actions. Secondary (2 px `outline`): cancel, less important choices. Grumpy: only the catch-up action while Poulpy is ember. A top bar's button is `sm`, a screen's or sheet's main action `xl`, a card's actions `md` |
| [Chip](?path=/docs/mobile-components-filter-chip--docs) | 32 high, pill; on = `primary`, off = 1.5 px `outline` | Filters (muscle, equipment, format), with `aria-pressed` or selected state; `PoulpyChip` on both platforms |
| Choosing one value | A select field that opens a sheet for a longer list, or a native menu for a short one | Pills and chips are for filters and for view switches; a form asks for a value with a select, never a row of pills |
| [Search field](?path=/docs/mobile-components-search-field--docs) | 44 high, pill, 1.5 px `outline`, `primary` while it holds text, a round clear button | `PillSearchField` on both platforms |
| [Stepper](?path=/docs/mobile-components-stepper--docs) | Inset `surfaceVariant` box, label, minus and plus round buttons of 44 | Sets, reps, load, rest |
| Pattern badge | 44 round, `primaryContainer`, one icon per movement pattern in `onPrimaryContainer` | Stands in for exercise pictures until real images exist |
| Filter row | Search field, a row of chips, then the count line with one action at its right | `ListFilters` on both platforms, shared by the exercise and workout lists |
| [Empty state](?path=/docs/mobile-patterns-empty-state--docs) | 14 radius, 2 px dashed `outline`, no fill. Poulpy thinking (64 px) beside a title and a line, then one small secondary button | `EmptyState`: no results, an empty list. Quiet on purpose: never a coloured card, never a big Poulpy |
| Item menu | A long press (iOS context menu) or ⋮; actions with icons, a divider, then the destructive one | Destructive items and their confirmation are `error` with an icon, never ember; the confirmation says what goes and what stays |
| Plan sheet | 14 radius sheet, a month from Monday; past days dimmed, today outlined, planned days `primary` | `PlanSheet` on both platforms; tick several days, save once |
| Error banner | `error` tint, icon plus text | Never ember |
| Loader | Poulpy's outline from underneath, drawn frame by frame from the 3D model with loose tentacles, swinging half a turn each way, once every 3.6 s; `clockwise` is the other version, which only turns one way | `PoulpyLoader` on both platforms; replaces spinners |


## Base fields

The fields every form is built from. Reference mockups: `profile` (guest), the `profile-*-sheet` pages, `body-log-sheet`, `body-log-height-sheet`, under Mobile > Screens > You > Profile and You > Profile sheets and body in the workshop. Build every new form from these, and move old screens to them as they are touched.

| Field | Shape | Behaviour |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Text field](?path=/docs/mobile-components-text-field--docs) | 52 high, 10 radius (`radius.md`), 1.5 px `outline`, label above, `primary` outline on focus | Free text: a name. A note under it in `onSurfaceVariant` |
| [Select field](?path=/docs/mobile-components-select-field--docs) | Same box, the chosen value (or a muted "Not set") and a chevron | Tapping opens a sheet; nothing else is shown on the screen. Never a row of pills |
| [Option sheet](?path=/docs/mobile-patterns-option-sheet--docs) | 14 radius sheet, a title, one row per choice | Single choice: a radio and the sheet closes on tap. Several choices: checkboxes and a Done button. A choice that needs text (Custom pronouns) shows its text field inside the sheet |
| [Number field](?path=/docs/mobile-components-number-field--docs) | Text field with a unit at its right (`bpm`, `kg`, `cm`) | Selects all its text on focus, opens the numeric keyboard, checks the range on leaving. Optional round − and + buttons on both sides (the `number-entry` pattern) for small nudges. Imperial height is two number fields, ft and in. Every numeric value in the app uses it, including the builder's sets, reps, rest between sets, rest and weight (`builder-sets-edit-step`, `builder-circuit-edit-step`); the − and + step by the same amount as before, and the typed value keeps the same limits |
| [Step slider](?path=/docs/mobile-components-step-slider--docs) | A bar of dots, `•--•--•--•--•`, the current label above it, the two ends named below | A fixed scale of five or fewer named steps, inside an option sheet |
| Password field (draft, #272) | Text field with a show or hide eye button inside its right end; a hint under it (`8 characters or more.`) | `password-field` in the mockups. Errors under a field are `error` text with an icon and the field outline turns `error`; a message about the whole form is an error card (`error` outline and text on `surfaceVariant`, with an icon) |
| [Line chart](?path=/docs/mobile-patterns-line-chart--docs) | Grid lines behind the line, the unit at the top of the axis, a value at each grid line, the date range under it | No grid colour that implies good or bad; a weight going up is never red |
| [Empty chart card](?path=/docs/mobile-patterns-empty-chart-card--docs) | The empty state's shape (dashed outline, no fill), a line and the one action that adds the first entry | Shown instead of the chart when nothing is logged |
| Body map | A front and a back figure, drawn in the build the person chose in the profile, as a muscle chart with transparent skin, side by side, each area a shape that fills with `primary` when picked; left and right are separate areas | Every area is a checkbox with a name ("Left knee"), so a screen reader and the text summary under the figure say the same. Left is the person's left: the right half of the front figure and the left half of the back one. Used to say where it hurts, and to show what an exercise works |
| Body map heat map ([#244](https://github.com/tkint/coach-poulpy/issues/244), built in #326) | The body map read-only for a whole workout: areas it doesn't work are outlines only, worked areas fill with `heat1`, `heat2` or `heat3` by sets per muscle group compared with the most-worked one (below 40 %, from 40 %, from 75 %); a worked area that hurts gets a 3 px `pain` outline on a `background` halo, drawn above its neighbours; a Less / More legend, with Hurts when something hurts | The figure is one image whose label is the text summary ("shoulders and triceps most, then chest, back and biceps, a little core"), and the heat steps differ in lightness, so nothing depends on colour alone. On the workout page; the format and equipment badges sit under the workout name in the sheet |
| Details sheet ([#244](https://github.com/tkint/coach-poulpy/issues/244), built in #326) | 14 radius sheet over the body map with no scrim; a handle, the workout name at its top, its content, then the action bar | Three stops (peek, half, full), the handle drags or taps between them: dragged down it shows only the name and actions under a full-screen map; half up by default (on the workout page it stops where the exercises start, so a half-open sheet never shows part of an exercise; the map takes the room that frees); full screen its content scrolls, and scrolling down past its top drags the sheet back down. The map keeps the size it has at half while the sheet goes higher. The back and menu buttons float above it at every stop |
| Plan summary ([#244](https://github.com/tkint/coach-poulpy/issues/244), built in #326) | `primaryContainer` row: a calendar icon, "3 sessions planned" over "Next on Fri 30 Oct", and a Change days button | Replaces one pill per planned day on the workout page |
