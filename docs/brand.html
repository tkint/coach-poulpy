Brand ​

Poulpy is a reversible plush octopus: blue and smiling on one side, grumpy on the other. In the app the grumpy side is ember, from burnt orange to red. The side is the status (are you keeping your plan?) and the shade is the feeling (what just happened). The full brand book, with the logo, colours and every drawing, is on the site's Brand book page, and Brand decisions traces what was decided and why.

Character rules ​

  • Grumpy is comic, never guilt. No "you failed", no streak-loss alarms. Even Fuming is a cartoon tantrum.
  • Pain and rehab always win. Someone who logged pain gets the Patched up pose and the blue side for 72 h, never ember.
  • One way back, always short. While Poulpy is ember, the catch-up offered is the shortest workout in the plan, and any finished session flips him straight back.
  • Nothing promised, nothing missed. With no plan, Poulpy stays on the blue side and only reacts to when you last trained.
  • Poulpy never lifts his tentacles. Every pose keeps the plush's shape and shows the mood with his face, shade, props and small effects.
  • At most one notification a day in Poulpy's voice, and turning them off keeps the mascot.
  • Every line in French and English from the start.
  • Inclusive by default. The app is LGBTQ+ friendly, and that is part of the brand: see Inclusive by default.

The ember side ​

Only a plan can turn Poulpy ember, through its calendar entries. A planned workout placed by hand is a reminder, not a promise, and never counts. See Brand decisions, decision 12.

  • Annoyed (the grace period). From 18:00 on an entry's day, while it is still planned, until the end of the next day. The entry can still be done the day after: finishing it then marks it done.
  • Grumpy. An entry still planned once the day after its day is over: it is missed.
  • Fuming. Two or more missed entries.
  • Any finished session flips him back. Entries dated on or before the day of the last finished session never count, whatever their status. Finishing the catch-up, a free session or another entry all work.
  • Only planned entries count. Done never counts; a moved entry counts on its new date only; a skipped entry never counts, because skipping is an honest choice and the ember side is for sessions left hanging.
  • Rest days never count. A plan day with no workout has no entry. On a rest day Poulpy sleeps if he is blue and stays ember if he is ember: a rest day doesn't erase a missed session.
  • Pain and rehab win. For 72 h after pain is logged or a rehab session is marked too hard, Poulpy is blue and Patched up, and entries dated inside those 72 h never count, even afterwards.
  • No enrollment, no ember. Without an active enrollment the blue-side rules apply.

When several apply, the first wins: pain or rehab, then Fuming, Grumpy, Annoyed, then the blue-side rules.

The catch-up is offered with Grumpy and Fuming, and nowhere on the blue side. It is the workout with the shortest estimated duration in the enrolled plan version, the earliest in the plan on a tie. Finishing it flips Poulpy back like any session; it leaves the missed entries as they are, for the person to skip or move. While Annoyed there is no catch-up: today's entry is the way back.

Inclusive by default ​

Poulpy is a plush with a blue side and a grumpy side, and nobody has to be one or the other. The app and everything around it welcomes LGBTQ+ people, and these rules apply to every screen, string, drawing and notification.

  • Ask only what a feature needs, and make every personal field optional. Each field says why it is asked and where the answer is used. "Prefer not to say" is always an option and never a dead end: the app works the same without it.
  • Sex and gender are two questions, and the app asks the first only. Where an estimate (calories, heart rate zones) depends on physiology, the field is called Sex assigned at birth, with Female, Male, Intersex and Prefer not to say, and a line saying it is not about gender. Avoid "biological sex", "birth sex" and "gender" for it. The app never asks about gender identity or orientation, and nothing it shows or says is decided by this field.
  • Pronouns are their own optional field, a select (choosing Custom shows a text field beneath it): She / her, He / him, They / them, Just my name, a custom entry the person types, or Prefer not to say. They only set French agreement and Poulpy's lines. Without an answer, or with Prefer not to say, the wording stays neutral, a custom entry is shown as written but never drives French agreement, and a pronoun is never guessed from a name, a photo or the sex field.
  • Neutral wording first. Write copy that needs no gender, in French too (« Bienvenue », « C'est fait », not « bienvenu(e) »). When agreement cannot be avoided, use the person's pronouns, and fall back to the neutral form.
  • Poulpy has no gender. He is a plush; he is "he" only in English by habit and "il" in French because poulpe is masculine. Never write a line that depends on who the person is, and never joke about bodies.
  • No body judgement. Weight, height and age are data. Poulpy never comments on a weight going up or down, charts use the one accent colour and never red or green for "worse" or "better", and the app shows no BMI and sets no weight goal unless the person asks.
  • Drawings and stories show a range of people. Future share cards, the website and store images show different bodies, ages, genders and families, and may use the rainbow only as a deliberate Pride moment, never as decoration on Poulpy.
  • Review before shipping. Wording about sex, gender or bodies is read by people from the community before release, and changed when they say so; the owner decides ties.

The reasons and the wording alternatives that were set aside are in Brand decisions. The profile fields that follow these rules are on Profile and settings.

Tentacles ​

Poulpy's ring has eight tentacles. Each finished session lights one, and a lit tentacle never goes dark: there is no weekly reset, so a quiet month takes nothing away. The eighth makes a full octopus (Champion), and the next session starts a new ring. This replaced "eight tentacles a week"; see Brand decisions, decision 11.

Voice ​

Poulpy speaks in short first-person lines, warm and a little cheeky: "6 of 8 tentacles lit. One more and I'm doing a little dance." or "Thursday's run is still waiting. 20 minutes and I flip back, promise." He jokes about himself, never about the person. In French he says tu, like a friend, while the rest of the app says vous.

Logo and type ​

The logo is the same drawing as the in-app Poulpy: a front view of the plush with five visible arms and the ember lining peeking out underneath. The app icon is that logo, happy and blue, on Foam (Deep sea for the iOS dark icon), generated by design/build-poulpy.mjs. In Settings › App › App icon a person can swap it for another face of Poulpy: Proud, Annoyed, Grumpy, Champion or Cocky, each the drawing the app already has, fitted into the icon. Choosing an icon is cosmetic; it never changes Poulpy's mood in the app. Headings use Baloo 2, body text Nunito; see the design system.

Ideas for later ​

  • Shells in the den: achievements as shells Poulpy collects (first 5 km, 10 sessions, a squat record).
  • Colourways: cosmetic seasonal skins unlocked by milestones.
  • Flip the real one: a widget or morning nudge saying which side the real plush should show today.
