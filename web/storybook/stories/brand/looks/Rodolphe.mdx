# Rodolphe, the red-nosed reindeer

**Draft.** A festive look for Poulpy to validate: nothing in the apps uses it yet, and no picture is generated from it. Poulpy dresses up as a red-nosed reindeer, our own take on the old idea and no one else's character, and he is very happy about it.

<Story of={Live.Rodolphe} />

- **A headband with felt antlers.** A slim brown band goes over the top of his head, leaning a little back, and two big soft antlers stand on it. Each is a fat beam that starts slim on the band, fills out and curls up and out, with three rounded tines. They are plush, not bone: round ends, no points.
- **A round, shiny red nose.** A bulb half out of his face, between his eyes and his smile, with a white dot of light like his eyes have. His happy face moves up a little to make room for it and his smile is a little smaller.
- **A white and red striped scarf.** Puffy knitted pieces all around the base of his head, sitting on his arms: lower in front, under his smile, higher behind. It is knotted over his front right arm and its two ends lie along that arm.
- **He sways to a tune.** A step to his left, a step to his right: his body turns a little each way on a spring and his arms trail behind it as they do when he turns, his head leans into each step and bobs on the beat, and the scarf's ends swing behind. Every eight beats he holds still for two, his nose still glowing.
- **His nose glows on the beat.** Every other beat it lights up, swells a hair and a warm glow blooms in front of it, then it fades back. The glow is light, so it belongs to whoever draws him, like the gleam; the model only holds the nose.

Turn the view and everything he wears goes round with him. The switch adds the outline; the speed slows the dance down or speeds it up.

## What stays true

- **Always the blue side, always happy.** Rodolphe is a treat, so he never wears the ember side and never frowns.
- **No arms.** His eight arms stay on the floor; the dance is in his body, his head and the scarf. The scarf's ends lie on an arm, they are not held.
- **His reds are not an alarm.** The nose and the scarf are a bright, warm cherry (`#F2353A`, `#E2373F`), lighter and yellower than the error crimson (`#C8264A`) and redder than any ember shade, and they only ever show on blue. Nothing about him reads as an error or as anger.
- **Flat, like the rest of him.** One shadow band, the plain colours below, no gloss: the nose's shine is a white dot, as on his eyes.

## Where it lives

[`design/poulpy-3d/looks/rodolphe.mjs`](https://github.com/tkint/coach-poulpy/blob/main/design/poulpy-3d/looks/rodolphe.mjs) holds the whole look, built like a pose and moving like a move: `FACE` and `COLOURS`, the shapes in `buildRodolphe()`, and `IDLE`, the dance's timing and springs, which `createRodolpheIdle()` runs. [`PoulpyRodolphe.vue`](https://github.com/tkint/coach-poulpy/blob/main/web/storybook/stories/brand/looks/PoulpyRodolphe.vue) draws it with the workshop's 3D scene and adds the glow.

**Still open:** whether he keeps the name Rodolphe; when he would show (a December look, a reward, a choice in settings); whether the dance is his idle or a move he does when tapped; and whether he goes into the glTF files and the pictures once agreed.
