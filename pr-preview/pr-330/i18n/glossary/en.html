English source: conventions and translator notes ​

English is the source language: every other language translates from it. This page holds the rules every translation follows and a note for each message whose meaning isn't obvious from the English alone. Fink shows only the id and the text, so look an id up here when in doubt.

How a message is written ​

  • Ids are <group>.<camelCaseName>: the group is the screen or area (library.sortPlanned, muscle.chest). Catalog ids are <slug>.<field>, with <slug>.instructions.<n> for each instruction in order. No id is the prefix of another.
  • Placeholders are named ICU arguments. {name} is text, {count, number} is a whole number. Keep every placeholder of the English, with the same name; move it wherever the sentence needs it.
  • Plurals are ICU plural: {count, plural, one {# session} other {# sessions}}. # is the number. Give every form your language needs (French: one, many, other); Fink lists them.
  • Apostrophes, ellipses and spaces need no care: type ' and ... as usual. After each merge they become ’ and …, and the non-breaking spaces a language needs are added (French: before : ; ! ? % and inside « »).
  • Product names stay as they are: Coach Poulpy, Poulpy.

Namespaces ​

Fink groups messages in three namespaces:

NamespaceWhat it holds
appThe app’s screens
domainWords shown everywhere: muscles, equipment, units, workout types, step roles, Poulpy’s lines
catalogExercise names, instructions, variation notes and search aliases

Translator notes ​

Messages not listed here mean what their English says.

app ​

IdNote
app.nameThe product name. Never translated.
common.loadingSpoken by screen readers while a screen loads; never seen.
tab.*Tab labels at the bottom of the screen: about 12 characters fit before the text shrinks. One word is best.
tab.liveThe tab for a session recorded from connected devices (treadmill, heart rate band), as it happens.
home.inDays{date} is a weekday and date, already formatted for the language.
library.sortiOS only: the label of the sort menu.
library.allFilter chip that clears the filter. Agrees with “workouts”.
library.planned, library.lastDone, workout.planned, history.todayDay{date} is a short date or “today”, already formatted.
exercise.variationPosition, exercise.variationsA11y{position} is already formatted, like “2 of 5”.
builder.less, builder.moreiOS only: buttons that lower or raise a value. Android uses builder.lessNamed/builder.moreNamed, where {label} names the value.
builder.open, builder.moreActionsAccessibility labels; {name} is the step’s name.
builder.editValueAccessibility label for typing a value; {label} names it (sets, weight…).
builder.repeat, builder.addRepeatA repeat is a group of steps done several times in a row, like intervals. Not “repetition” of a single movement (that is a rep).
builder.untilPressed, builder.untilEndA step with no set length: it lasts until the person presses the button, or, on the last step, until the session ends.
player.summary{duration} is already formatted, like “24 min”.
treadmill.phaseA block is one part of a treadmill program, like “5 min at 8 km/h”.
treadmill.targetSpeed, treadmill.remaining{speed} and {time} are already formatted with their unit.
history.sessionsUnitThe word alone, shown under a big number.
history.tentaclesLitOut of Poulpy’s eight tentacles; see the Poulpy notes below.
live.waitingBody, live.connectingOne, devices.forgetTitle, devices.usedInstead{name} is a device’s name.
live.finishStillBody, live.finishStoppedBody, live.finishLostBody{endTime} is a clock time, already formatted.
live.finishClosedBody{endTime} is how far into the session it stopped, like “12:40”, already formatted.
live.notificationPaused{elapsed} is the time recorded so far, like “12:40”.
devices.openSettingsiOS only: opens the phone’s Settings app. Use the name the phone uses.
devices.allowBluetoothAndroid only.
kind.footPodA small sensor clipped to the shoe that measures pace and cadence.
settings.languageNextLaunchiOS only.
profile.sex*The “sex assigned at birth” wording is deliberate (decision 13): never “biological sex” or “birth sex”, and never gender. Keep it as a question about what was recorded, not about the person.
profile.pronoun*The options of a select. In French the options are the pronouns themselves (Elle, Il, Iel), not “she / her” translated.
profile.pronounsNoteA custom entry is shown as the person typed it and is never translated or corrected.
body.range1m, body.range3m, body.range1y, body.rangeAllChart ranges: 1 month, 3 months, 1 year, all. A few characters only.
body.changeThe difference between the first and last entry of the range. Neutral: going up or down is never good or bad.
body.chartSpoken description of the chart. {first} and {last} are weights or heights with their unit, the dates are already formatted.

domain ​

IdNote
zone.*Body zones of the exercise filter, with left and right together; shorter than bodyArea.*.
pattern.*Movement patterns, the families exercises are grouped in. pattern.hinge bends at the hips with a flat back. pattern.carry walks holding a load.
role.*The tag on a step in a workout. role.recover is easy effort between hard efforts, not a full stop.
label.sessionShown for a session whose workout was deleted.
label.or, label.andJoin filter labels: “Dumbbells or kettlebell”.
label.demoIntervalsThe name of a built-in treadmill program for trying the app.
count.position“3 of 5”, used in many places.
unit.*Unit formats. unit.minutesSeconds and unit.hoursMinutes: the second argument is already two digits (“1 h 05”).
workout.deleteTitle{name} is the workout’s name, chosen by the person.
poulpy.*Poulpy, the octopus mascot, speaking in the first person. He lights one of his eight tentacles per finished session; eight make a full octopus. He is warm and never guilt-trips. His voice is described on the brand page.

catalog ​

IdNote
<slug>.aliasesOther names people might search for, comma-separated, never shown. Optional per language: add the words people really type, not a translation of the English list.
<slug>.variationNoteHow this variation differs from its neighbours, shown under the name.
<slug>.instructions.<n>One step of how to do the exercise, in order, addressed to the person.
