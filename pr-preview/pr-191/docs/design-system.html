Design system ​

The app is calm and rounded, like the plush: one blue accent for actions, white or near-white surfaces, and only Poulpy and the Home header tint with his mood. Values live in design/tokens.json and are generated into each platform (see design/README.md); this page says how to use them. The live swatches are on the site's Tokens page.

Colour roles ​

Use roles, never raw palette steps, in screens. Each role has a light and a dark value.

RoleUse it for
primary / onPrimaryThe main action on a screen (Start session, Save), selected chips, links, progress
primaryContainer / onPrimaryContainerTonal buttons, selected rows, Poulpy's speech on the blue side
grumpy / onGrumpyThe one action offered while Poulpy is on the ember side (Catch up, Flip him back)
grumpyContainer / onGrumpyContainerThe Home header and cards while Poulpy is on the ember side
background, surface, surfaceVariantScreen, cards and sheets, inset fields and steppers
onSurfaceVariant, outlineSecondary text and captions; card borders and dividers
successA confirmed save or a finished phase, with a check icon
error / onErrorErrors only: crimson, always with an icon and words

Rules:

  • poulpy.blue, poulpy.ember and poulpy.ink are Poulpy's fabric and never change between light and dark.
  • The plush blue and ember are too light for white text: buttons use primary and grumpy.
  • Ember means "a session slipped", never "something broke". An error is crimson, so a cross Poulpy is never read as a failure.
  • Colours that must be told apart also differ in lightness, not hue alone.

Mood shades ​

Six shades, from mood.*, only on Poulpy himself, the Home header tint and mood-coloured charts:

SideShades, calmest to strongest
Blue (on track)recovering, happy, proud
Ember (a session slipped)annoyed, grumpy, fuming

Which shade shows when is in Brand.

Type ​

Baloo 2 for display and titles, Nunito for everything else. Platforms map each role to a system text style so Dynamic Type and font scaling keep working (.font(.poulpy(.headline)) on iOS, MaterialTheme.typography on Android).

RoleFontSize / lineUse
displayBaloo 2 80040 / 44Poulpy's line on Home, end-of-session headline
titleBaloo 2 70028 / 32Screen titles, workout name on its page
headlineBaloo 2 70021 / 25Section heads, the current exercise in the player
bodyNunito 50016 / 24Everything you read
labelNunito 800, tracked 0.08 em, upper case12.5 / 16Eyebrows, tab labels, "Next up"
timerNunito 800, tabular figures64 / 64Timers and rep counts, which must not jitter

Words never break. Translations run longer than English (« Bibliothèque », « Échauffement »), so text is laid out for any length:

  • Text in a space of fixed width (tab labels, chips, stat tiles, stepper values) stays on one line and shrinks to fit, never below 9 sp / pt; past that it ends in an ellipsis. FitText on Android, .lineLimit(1).minimumScaleFactor(…) on iOS, whose tab bar already does it.
  • Buttons wrap their label between words rather than shrink it.
  • Every other text wraps between words only, never inside one, and never hyphenates.

Space, radius, motion ​

  • Spacing steps: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48 (xs to xxxl). Pick a step; never a value between them.
  • Radius: 10 for chips and small fields, 16 for cards, 24 for sheets, pill for buttons.
  • Motion: the flip (700 ms with overshoot) is the one signature move, used only when Poulpy changes side. Mood shades fade in 300 ms. Everything else is 150 to 250 ms and plain. With reduced motion on, the loader slows to one turn in 10 s.

Screens ​

Mockups are plain HTML pages in design/mockups/ (ADR 0016): drafts in drafts/, validated screens in agreed/, each split into phone/, tablet/ and web/. The docs site shows them on the Mockups pages, one page per screen, in light or dark. Build a screen from its agreed mockup, and get a mockup agreed, through a PR that moves it out of drafts/, before building a screen that has none. The player draws the same screens on both phones (player-ready, player-set, player-circuit-round, player-repeat-in-sets, player-warm-up, player-rest, player-recover): pause and end on the bar over the step count, Poulpy straining on an exercise and glancing aside inside the rest ring, a rep target as one big number with its unit under it, a line saying what comes next, and −15 s and +15 s under the ring to lengthen or shorten that one rest. The screen sits on the background role, not surface, so the cards on it stand out. The finished screen names the workout and its date, then shows three stats picked from what the exercises logged: Time always, then sets and weight lifted for sets and weights, distance and pace for runs and walks, and never heart rate; a stat with nothing logged, such as the weight of a bodyweight session, is left out (player-finished and player-finished-run on the Mockups pages). CI runs node design/check-mockups.mjs on every mockup: no literal colours, no px value where a spacing, radius or headline token has that size, no missing link, no script or style from outside the repo (the brand fonts apart), and the title, screen and section meta tags in place.

Accessibility ​

  • Touch targets at least 44 by 44.
  • Text contrast at least 4.5:1, including small grey labels; 3:1 only for text 24 px and up.
  • Every icon-only button has a label; decorative drawings of Poulpy are hidden from screen readers, and a pose that carries meaning has a description.
  • Nothing depends on colour alone: mood changes also change Poulpy's face and words, errors carry an icon.
