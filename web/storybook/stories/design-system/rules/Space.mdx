# Space, radius and motion

## Space, radius, motion

- Spacing steps: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48 (`xs` to `xxxl`). Pick a step; never a value between them.
- Radius: 8 for chips and small fields, 10 for cards, rows and buttons, 14 for sheets, pill for chips. Small cards stay close to square so their text reads first.
- Controls stay compact: a button is 40 high by default and follows the size scale of the [Button](?path=/docs/mobile-components-button--docs) (xs 24 to xxl 64), an icon button 40; below 44 the touch area stays 44. The side gutter of a screen is 16, and rows are 56 high with a 32 to 40 avatar. In the apps these come from one place, `PoulpySize` (generated from `design/tokens.json`, next to `PoulpySpace` and `PoulpyRadius`); a screen never writes a literal height, gutter or corner radius.
- Motion: the flip (700 ms with overshoot) is the one signature move, used only when Poulpy changes side. Mood shades fade in 300 ms. Everything else is 150 to 250 ms and plain. With reduced motion on, the loader slows to one swing in 12 s.
