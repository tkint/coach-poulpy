# Poulpy and the brand

Poulpy is a friendly, reversible plush octopus: blue and smiling by default, with a playful grumpy mood on his ember side. In the app, ember shifts from burnt orange to red as that mood intensifies. **The side is the status** (are you keeping your plan?) **and the shade is the feeling** (what just happened). These pages hold the rules, every drawing, Poulpy in 3D and the full brand book, with the logo and colours, and [Brand decisions](?path=/docs/brand-decisions--docs) traces what was decided and why.
