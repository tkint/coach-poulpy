Poulpy is a friendly, reversible plush octopus: blue and smiling by default, with a playful grumpy mood on his ember side. The side is the status and the shade is the feeling. His moods and poses are pictures painted from the 3D model by `design/build-poulpy-pictures.mjs` into `design/poulpy-3d/pictures/`, transparent so they sit on a light or a dark screen; the loader is a drawing generated by `design/build-poulpy.mjs` into `design/poulpy/`. The website uses these files; the apps still draw the older drawings until they switch over.

A **mood** is Poulpy's face and shade for what just happened (Proud, Happy, Recovering are blue; Annoyed, Grumpy, Fuming are ember). A **pose** shows an activity and keeps whatever mood he is in. Pain and rehab always show the blue side; Poulpy is never guilt. The rules for when each drawing shows are in the Brand book; the web component that draws them is under Web, Components, Display, Poulpy.
