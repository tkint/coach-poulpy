# Poulpy fuming

The ember side at its angriest. A draft for Home, to validate before anything is baked for the phones: how he answers a tap while he is already fuming, with no transition. He is fuming when the page opens, his fumes drifting; tap him in the picture, or pick a variant.

<Story of={Live.Fuming} />

- **He tucks a little and comes back to standing**, the fumes moving harder meanwhile. The three variants differ in how deep he goes, how far his arms widen and whether he pops back up or shudders.
- **The fumes** are the flat puffs of the fuming pose, bobbing and drifting, and faster while he tucks.
- The tucks are the first part of [a little jump](?path=/docs/brand-poulpy-moves-little-jumps--docs), kept on the floor.

How he gets here and how he calms down are on [Getting angry](?path=/docs/brand-poulpy-moves-getting-angry--docs). Pain and rehab never show the ember side, so they never come in here.
