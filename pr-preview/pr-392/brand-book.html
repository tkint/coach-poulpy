Coach Poulpy · brand book

One octopus, two moods.

Poulpy is a reversible plush: blue and smiling on one side, grumpy on the other. In the app his grumpy side turns ember, from burnt orange to red, and both sides shift shade with how he feels.

Tap Poulpy to flip him.

Blue side: you trained, Poulpy is happy.

2 · Colour

Blue when it goes well, ember when it doesn't

The grumpy side is a warm ember: burnt orange (#D9622B) for everyday grumpiness, deepening into red when Poulpy gets really cross.

The mood spectrum

Poulpy's colour follows his emotion, not just his side. The blue side stays close to the plush: a touch richer when proud, lighter and fresher while recovering. Missed sessions heat the ember up toward red. Tap a shade.

Light theme roles

Dark theme roles

With a red-leaning mascot, error messages move to a crimson (#C8264A) and always come with an icon and text, so a cross Poulpy is never mistaken for an error. The plush ember (#D9622B) is too light for white text, so buttons use the deeper ember 600.

8 · Gamification

Ideas built from real octopus facts

Core ideas come first; the others can join later.

The flip

Core

Poulpy's side follows the mood rules above. Coming back is always one session away, and the flip animation plus a haptic tap is the reward for it.

Inspired by the plush itself.

Eight tentacles

Core

The ring has eight tentacles. Each finished session lights one, and a lit tentacle never goes dark: there is no weekly reset. The eighth gives the Champion pose and a "full octopus", and the next session starts a new ring.

Octopuses have eight tentacles, and Poulpy never grows more.

Mood shades

Core

Poulpy's colour moves along the spectrum with each emotion: a touch richer after a record, lighter while recovering, hotter ember the longer a session waits.

Octopuses change colour with their mood.

Shells in the den

Later

Achievements are shells Poulpy collects in his den: first 5 km on the treadmill, 10 sessions, a squat record, a full rehab block finished.

Octopuses decorate their dens with shells and stones.

Colourways

Maybe

Unlock seasonal skins for Poulpy after milestones (a coral reef edition, a deep-sea night edition). Purely cosmetic.

Octopuses can camouflage in a fraction of a second.

Flip the real one

Maybe

A home-screen widget and an optional morning nudge that says which side the real plush on your desk should show today.

Only works because the real Poulpy exists.

Ground rules for Poulpy

  • Grumpy is comic, never guilt. No "you failed", no streak-loss alarms. Even Fuming is a cartoon tantrum.
  • Pain and rehab always win: a hurt user gets the Patched up pose, never the ember side.
  • One way back, always short: the catch-up session offered while ember is the easiest one in the plan.
  • Notifications speak in Poulpy's voice at most once a day, and can be turned off without losing the mascot.
  • All of Poulpy's lines are written in French and English from the start.
