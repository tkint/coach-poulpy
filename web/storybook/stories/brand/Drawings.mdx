# Drawings

Poulpy's six mood portraits, his poses and the loader, as generated by [`design/build-poulpy.mjs`](https://github.com/tkint/coach-poulpy/blob/main/design/build-poulpy.mjs). Portraits and poses share one 248 × 226 frame, so they can replace each other without moving. When each one shows is on the [Moods](?path=/docs/brand-moods--docs#when-each-drawing-shows) page. The same drawings, as the apps use them, are under [Core > Poulpy](?path=/docs/core-poulpy--docs).
