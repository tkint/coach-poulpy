# Body map shapes

The muscle outlines in `parts.json` come from [hugobonifay/body-map](https://github.com/hugobonifay/body-map) (commit `42957b15338ae6410c0e6a1380e062d4a50c10f3`), which declares the MIT licence in its README. `build-body-map.mjs` redraws them for Coach Poulpy: it converts them to plain curves, keeps the shared muscle names, stretches the two builds, and leaves the skin transparent. Please keep this notice with the shapes.
