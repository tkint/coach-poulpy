# Poulpy fuming

The ember side at its angriest, moving. This is a draft for Home, to validate before anything is baked for the phones: the three moves that belong to the fuming Poulpy, each with variants to compare. The whole story, from his normal self to fuming and back, is on [Getting angry](?path=/docs/brand-poulpy-moves-getting-angry--docs).

<Story of={Live.Fuming} />

- **Going fuming.** From annoyed he turns once on the spot and comes out fuming, with his fumes puffing behind him.
- **Tapped while fuming.** He tucks a little and comes back to standing, the fumes moving harder meanwhile. Tapping him in the picture does the same, with the variant chosen.
- **Calming down.** After a while he turns back the other way, clockwise, to annoyed, and the fumes go.

The turns are exactly 360 degrees. On the spot, with a hop and wind-up and whip are the move of [Turning](?path=/docs/brand-poulpy-moves-turning--docs) and [Jumping around](?path=/docs/brand-poulpy-moves-jumping-around--docs) with their values fixed; the tucks are the first part of [a little jump](?path=/docs/brand-poulpy-moves-little-jumps--docs), kept on the floor.

Pain and rehab never show the ember side, so they never come in here.
